Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains how the upload automation (the "App") for ClipMoa, a YouTube Shorts channel, handles Google account data.

1. Operator

  • Operator: ClipMoa operator (클립모아 운영자)
  • Contact: keullibmoa@gmail.com

2. Purpose

The App is used only to upload Shorts videos created by the operator to the operator's own YouTube channel. It is not a service offered to the general public.

3. Google permission used

The App requests only one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload videos to a YouTube channel

The App does not access watch history, subscriptions, email, contacts, or any other data.

4. Data collected and storage

  • Data collected: only the OAuth token issued after Google sign-in.
  • Use: solely to upload videos to the operator's channel.
  • Storage: kept only in secure storage used by the operator's automation (GitHub Actions encrypted secrets).
  • Sharing: never sold, transferred, or shared with any third party, and never used for advertising or analytics.

5. How to delete your data

You can revoke access and request deletion at any time in either of these ways:

  • Remove ClipMoa under Third-party access (Third-party apps & services) in your Google Account. (Open)
  • Email keullibmoa@gmail.com to request deletion. We will delete the stored token and confirm by reply.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

ClipMoa's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. YouTube and Google terms

The App uses YouTube API Services. Videos uploaded through the App are subject to the following:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • YouTube API Services Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version and its effective date on this page.
